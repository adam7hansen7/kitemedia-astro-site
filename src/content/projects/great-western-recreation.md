<h2>Details</h2>
<p>Great Western Recreation does a lot more than just playground equipment. From park planning and design to athletic courts, splash pads, outdoor fitness equipment, and complete park installations, they offer just about everything communities need to create awesome outdoor spaces. We partnered with them to build a website that showcases all that they do and makes it easy for potential customers to explore their options.</p>
<p>We designed a modern, organized website where visitors can browse products, learn about available services, explore completed projects, and get to know the people behind the company. We also built out dedicated sections for equipment details, project galleries, team information, and service areas, giving visitors a much better picture of what Great Western Recreation brings to the table.</p>
