<h2>Details</h2>
<p><span>We've worked with Beaver Creek Lodge for years, originally building their website back when they first partnered with us. This time around, it was time for a refresh with a new look. Located in one of our favorite areas, Logan Canyon, just a short drive from Bear Lake and Beaver Mountain, Beaver Creek Lodge offers a unique mix of lodging, snowmobile and side by side rentals, guided adventures, and an amazing wedding and event venue.</span></p>
<p><span>We wanted the new website to capture just how awesome this place really is. We went with bolder colors, stronger typography, and plenty of great photography and video to show off the lodge, surrounding scenery, and outdoor experiences. We also organized everything into easy-to-navigate sections so visitors can explore lodging options, equipment rentals, adventure packages, weddings, and more.</span></p>
