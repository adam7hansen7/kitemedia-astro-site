<h2>Details</h2>
<p>EB Hoops Academy is a youth basketball training program based in Cache Valley, Utah and run by our friend and former division 1 basketball coach, Eric Brown. He built the program around growing skills, confidence, and love for the game. We're basketball junkies at Kite Media so this project was a perfect fit — we teamed up to give EB Hoops a site that matches Eric's energy, mission, and vision for developing young athletes.</p>
<p>We delivered a clean, easy-to-navigate site that showcases what EB Hoops offers: skills training, camps, clinics, and workouts that will enable young hoopers to become more competitive. The design emphasizes clarity and action: from viewing program options and learning about the training philosophy, to easily finding how to register or get in touch. Strong visuals, simple layouts, and friendly messaging make it clear right away that EB Hoops is about community, growth, and fun.</p>
<p>The website also reinforces trust and professionalism with testimonials from many of Eric's past players — including several well-known pros and local legends — giving parents and players confidence that EB Hoops is reliable and serious about quality training. By balancing excitement for basketball with clear, accessible info, we helped create a digital presence that feels authentic and inviting. In addition to the website design &amp; build, we captured photography, created a short video, designed the logo, and will provide continued graphic design support such as flyers and social media graphics for upcoming camps and clinics.</p>
<p>At the end of the day, EB Hoops Academy now has a website that not only reflects their passion for the game — it helps them reach more families, connect with our local community, and grow their youth-training program.</p>
<p>Kite Media was tremendous to work with. Their expertise, knowledge and attention to detail is first class. They have an extremely talented team who made me feel like a priority. They answer and address every question with a detailed plan. The creativity and ability to make my website stand out was elite. I highly recommend Kite Media to anyone who wants to have a top of the line and high quality product.</p>
