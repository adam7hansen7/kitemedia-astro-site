<p>Koerner Law is a specialized law firm providing multijurisdictional legal counsel and structural advisory to institutional investors and alternative investment clients worldwide. We designed and developed a modern WordPress website that reflects the firm’s sophisticated, forward-thinking approach, using a clean visual system, bold typography, and geometric shapes to create a distinctive and professional digital presence.</p>
<p>When launching Koerner Law, I needed a web developer who could translate a specific vision into a highly polished, professional digital presence. Adam at Kite Media delivered beyond my expectations.</p>
<p>I provided a few sample sites and a general color motif, and he took that limited instruction and executed the concept perfectly. What impressed me most was his initiative; he conducted his own research to fill in the gaps and worked highly independently without needing constant oversight.</p>
<p>Furthermore, he was incredibly accommodating of my timeline. He prioritized critical tasks to ensure we hit our hard launch deadline, gracefully adjusting his workflow even when I caused a few delays on my end.</p>
<p>Adam is a talented, intuitive, and highly professional developer, and I cannot recommend Kite Media enough.</p>
