<h2>Details</h2>
<p>We're beyond happy with how this website design project turned out for our buddies at MOB Racing, a race club in Lehi, Utah. It's always great to work with good friends, especially when they give you free reign to design a site that so closely aligns with your own interests and style. To kick off the project, they showed us their race kits, a general color scheme, examples of other websites they liked, and trusted us with a green light to run with it from there. They wanted a website that potential &amp; current team members could use to get all of the information they needed in one place. We took their initial input &amp; ran with it, creating a site with a dark color scheme &amp; bright pops of color, monochrome design touches, subtle animations, amazing fullwidth photography, and large, bold text. The site features an event calendar, information about the club, their story, and contact forms to get in touch. All said &amp; done, it's a site that we're proud to put our name on.</p>
<p>To add to that, MOB's purpose is something we were excited to get behind. The term “MOB” in aboriginal culture describes a family, group, or community who are united in purpose. It takes on double meaning, with the acronym of mind over body. It's with this vision in mind, that their purpose &amp; mission emerged. MOB Racing focuses on opportunities to give back, providing opportunities for others to experience better mental &amp; physical health, and overall better well-being with group support. The club consists of individuals who train &amp; compete in cycling, running, and swimming races throughout Utah.</p>
<p>Cool people. Cool purpose. And now a cool website.</p>
