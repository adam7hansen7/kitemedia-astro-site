<h2>Details</h2>
<p>Van America is based in St. George, UT and builds premium adventure vans designed for performance, comfort, and life on the road. They came to our team at Kite Media looking for a website that would not only showcase their craftsmanship, but also streamline the customer journey for both their production models and fully custom van builds. They were already deep into the process with another web design company, but the project was taking way too long and the supposed "web designer" was actually just outsourcing all of the work to a company overseas, which led to delayed turnaround and poor communication. This frustrating experience led them to seek out a new web designer in St. George that would keep the design work in-house, provide great communication, and fair pricing - and that's where we came in.</p>
<p>We created a modern, visually immersive website that highlights Van America’s expertise, premium materials, and brand. Large, high-quality imagery and clean layouts allow each van model to shine. Detailed build pages, an intuitive navigation structure, and clear calls-to-action help visitors easily compare models, explore features, and begin the inquiry process.</p>
<p>To support Van America’s credibility in a competitive market, we also built dedicated content to highlight their safety certifications, build philosophy, FAQs, and their approach to craftsmanship. These reinforce trust and give customers the confidence they need when considering a high-investment purchase.</p>
<p>The new site positions Van America as a leader in the adventure-van space by blending lifestyle appeal with technical authority. With improved usability, stronger storytelling, and a more conversion-focused structure, Van America now has an online presence that reflects the quality and ambition of their brand — and helps them turn curious explorers into committed customers.</p>
