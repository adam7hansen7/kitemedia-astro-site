<h2>Details</h2>
<p>We recently wrapped up a new website for our good friends at Stack Built, a residential and commercial developer building homes and developments across Utah, Idaho, and Wyoming. They wanted a modern website that reflected the quality of their work, showcased their brand, and gave potential clients a better look at what they do.</p>
<p>We paired clean design with engaging animations, high-quality photography, and bold typography to bring everything together. Beyond simply presenting information, we wanted to give visitors a feel for the Stack Built brand, their team, and their style. The site includes a portfolio of completed projects, a dedicated section for current property listings, and well-organized pages covering their services, team, and contact information.</p>
<p>The finished website strikes a balance between style and functionality. It's visually engaging, easy to navigate, and gives Stack Built a place to show off their work and connect with potential clients.</p>
