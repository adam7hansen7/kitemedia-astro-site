<h2>Details</h2>
<p>The Paddle Pickleball is a new indoor pickleball facility in Hyrum, Utah, offering memberships, court reservations, events, and community play. They partnered with our team at Kite Media to create a website that would establish their brand, show off their space, and make it easy for players to get involved from day one.</p>
<p>We designed a clean, modern website that captures the energy and community feel of The Paddle. Bold imagery, clear calls-to-action, and streamlined navigation guide visitors through everything the facility offers — from membership tiers and punch passes to court reservations and sponsorship opportunities. The site was built to grow with the business, giving The Paddle a digital home that feels complete, professional, and player-friendly, with the ability to add on at any time down the road as needed.</p>
<p>The new website gives The Paddle Pickleball a strong online presence that reflects the quality and vibe of their awesome new facility. The Paddle now has a website that helps them attract new players, support their members, and build a thriving local pickleball community in Cache Valley. If you're a local pickleballer, be sure to check them out!</p>
