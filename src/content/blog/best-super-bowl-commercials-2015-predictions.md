2015 Predictions
<p>In today's day and age most companies have already released their ads before the Super Bowl and there are few surprises left, but there are a few companies that have only hinted at what is going to happen and these are the ads we think will be the best.</p>
<h4>Skittles</h4>
<p>Skittles has always had awesome commercials. From the "<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3Zma2_n5CA">Skittles Touch</a>" to "<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F9R6leCagXs">Skittles Cloud</a>," they've got some creative ideas. They have been promoting their new commercial since early January with retired quarterback Kurt Warner. This will be the first year they have a Super Bowl commercial and they definitely won't disappoint.</p>
<h4>Doritos</h4>
<p>For the past 9 years, Doritos has featured consumer generated ads, and this year will be no different. They will be featuring two 30-second ads. Doritos has narrowed it down to 10 finalists and you can see them all <a href="https://crashthesuperbowl.doritos.com/finalists">here</a>.</p>
<h4>Nationwide Insurance</h4>
<p>Nationwide has released a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P30yUzwSTKo">teaser ad featuring Mindy Kaling</a>. There are a lot of ads from Insurance companies each year, so hopefully Nationwide can do something creative.</p>
<p>For a full breakdown of coming ads in the Super Bowl, take a look at <a href="http://adage.com/article/special-report-super-bowl/super-bowl-xlix-ad-chart-buying-big-game-commercials/295841/">Ad Age's article</a>.</p>
