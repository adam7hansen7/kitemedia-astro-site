<h2>Is your site mobile-friendly?</h2>
<p>On April 21, Google Search expanded its use of mobile-friendliness as a search ranking signal for websites. Double-check your website is up to par for this change by testing pages of your site with Google's Mobile-Friendly Test tool.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.google.com/webmasters/tools/mobile-friendly/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Click Here To Take The Mobile-Friendly Test</a></p>
<p>This change will affect mobile searches in all languages worldwide and will have a significant impact in Google Search results. Users will find it easier to get relevant, high quality search results optimized for their devices.<br/>
<img alt="" loading="lazy" src="https://services.google.com/fh/files/emails/mobile-friendly1.jpg"/></p>
<p> </p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>By being mobile-friendly, you’ll benefit too by growing your audience across all screens.</strong></p>
<h3>My site’s already mobile optimized: do I need to take action?</h3>
<p><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="https://services.google.com/fh/files/emails/mobilefriendly12.jpg"/>Even if you already have a mobile-optimized site, it’s worth taking some time to double-check your site’s pages using the Mobile-Friendly Test tool. Also, check out the AdSense Multiscreen Implementation Guide, which covers AdSense implementation best practices to improve monetization on mobile.</p>
<p> </p>
<h3>My site isn’t mobile-friendly: how do I get started?</h3>
<p><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="https://services.google.com/fh/files/emails/mobile-friendly3.jpg"/>The guide to mobile-friendly sites can help you get started, while you get a full list of mobile usability issues across your sites by logging in to your Webmaster Tools account and using the Mobile Usability Report. You can also check out the AdSense Multi-Screen Starter Guide and post questions to Webmaster Central Forums to get support.</p>
<p> </p>
<p><a href="https://www.google.com/webmasters/tools/mobile-friendly/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Take Mobile-Friendly Test</a></p>
<p>Mobile is critical to your business and will continue to be so. Make sure visitors can have a good experience on your site when they’re visiting from their mobile devices.</p>
<p>*These suggestions are designed to help you improve your pages and don’t guarantee any specific results. And, remember, you are responsible for the content and layout of your website.</p>
