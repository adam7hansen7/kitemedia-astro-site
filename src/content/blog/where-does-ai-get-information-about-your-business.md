<p>Ask ChatGPT, Claude, or Google's AI answers to recommend a business like yours, and they'll give an answer that sounds confident. But where does it come from? That’s the question many small businesses are asking.</p>
<p>None of these tools publishes a complete list of sources, and the details change often. The pattern is consistent, though: they generally pull from what's already out there about your business. If that information is thin, outdated, or inconsistent, the AI's answer will be too. Here are the places that matter most for businesses that want to impact how their brand shows up in AI results.</p>
<h2>1. Your Website</h2>
<p><a href="/services/website-design/">Your website</a> is the one source you fully control, and AI tools lean on it heavily. They read your pages to learn what you do, where you work, and what makes you different.</p>
<p><strong>What helps:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Clear service pages that say exactly what you offer and who it's for</li>
<li>Location or service area pages with real details, not thin copy</li>
<li>Up-to-date contact information, hours, and an About page that shows who's behind the business</li>
<li>Structured data (schema markup) that labels your business details in a way machines can read</li>
<li>Clear answers to common questions users might be searching for, especially in a question-and-answer format that is easy for AI to understand</li>
<li>A site that loads quickly and isn't blocking search crawlers. It's worth confirming your robots.txt file isn't accidentally keeping out the crawlers AI search tools use</li>
</ul>
<h2>2. Your Google Business Profile</h2>
<p>For local businesses, your Google Business Profile is a major source of facts: your name, address or service area, hours, categories, photos, and reviews. Google's own AI features draw on Google's data, and other tools that search the web often surface it too.</p>
<p><strong>What helps:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Complete every section, choose accurate categories, keep hours current (including holidays), etc</li>
<li>Regularly update your profile by checking information, adding new posts, new photos, etc</li>
<li>Make sure this information matches the other information about your business across the web.</li>
</ul>
<h2>3. Reviews</h2>
<p>Reviews tell AI tools what it's actually like to hire you. They pick up on the language customers use, such as "on time," "fair price," or "great with pets," and the overall sentiment. These are keywords customers search for or care about that don’t naturally pop up elsewhere.</p>
<p><strong>What helps:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>A steady flow of recent reviews on Google and on the other platforms your customers use, not waves of reviews and gaps in between</li>
<li>Thoughtful replies to both the good and the bad reviews</li>
</ul>
<h2>4. Directories and Listings</h2>
<p>Sites like Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook, and industry-specific directories all hold information about your business. When those listings disagree with each other (an old phone number here, a wrong address there), AI tools have conflicting facts to choose from, and they may pick the wrong one.</p>
<p><strong>What helps:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Consistent name, address, phone number, website, and other business information like services offered, across every listing</li>
<li>Periodic checks for duplicates or outdated entries. Software or an <a href="/our-team/">SEO company</a> can help manage this for busy business owners</li>
</ul>
<h2>5. What Other People Say About You</h2>
<p>AI tools don't only listen to what you say about yourself. News articles, local guides, community pages, partner websites, and "best of" lists all shape how a business is described. A business mentioned by trusted third parties is easier for an AI to recommend with confidence.</p>
<p><strong>What helps:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Local press, sponsorships, partnerships, community involvement, and being listed on respected industry and local sites</li>
<li>Local awards and honors, such as local “best of” awards and being featured in top lists can be a big boost for businesses</li>
</ul>
<h2>6. Direct submissions and AI Tools</h2>
<p>AI platforms are beginning to offer businesses more ways to manage how their information appears and understand how they're being discovered. For example, OpenAI recently introduced <a href="https://chatgpt.com/business-hub">Business Hub</a>, a beta feature that allows eligible businesses to claim their profile in ChatGPT, update key business details, and access insights about how their business appears in ChatGPT.</p>
<p>As AI platforms continue to evolve, expect more tools and features that give businesses opportunities to manage their presence and improve how they appear in AI-powered search results.</p>
<p><strong>What helps:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Explore business tools and profile management options offered by ChatGPT and other AI platforms.</li>
<li>Keep an eye on new features and updates that give businesses more control over their information or insights into how customers find them.</li>
<li>Take advantage of opportunities to verify your business, update details, and provide accurate information directly to AI platforms when available.</li>
</ul>
<p>This is a rapidly changing area of digital marketing, so staying informed can help your business take advantage of new opportunities as they become available.</p>
<h2>How to see what AI says about your business</h2>
<p>You don't need special software to get a first read. Try this:</p>
<ol>
<li>Ask a few AI tools the questions your customers would ask, such as "best [your service] in [your city]" and "is [your business] any good?"</li>
<li>Note whether you're mentioned, what's said, and which sources or links are cited.</li>
<li>Check the facts: services, service area, hours, phone number.</li>
<li>Repeat monthly, since answers change over time.</li>
</ol>
<p>If something is wrong, trace it back. Usually the error lives on a listing, a profile, or an old page you can fix.</p>
<h2>Information, not shortcuts</h2>
<p>There's no switch that forces an AI tool to recommend you. What you can do is make sure the information about your business is accurate, consistent, and easy to find across your website, your profiles, your reviews, and the wider web.</p>
