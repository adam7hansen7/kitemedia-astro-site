<p>There aren't any magic secrets to getting your site to the top of Google instantly. However, if you are looking for quick steps that can help <a href="/services/search-engine-optimization/">improve your online presence</a>, here’s our list of things you can do right now. </p>
<h2>Be Consistent</h2>
<p>Make sure everywhere your business is listed online has the same information. Look at your business name, address, phone numbers, hours, and other information to make sure that is consistent on your website, Google listing, and other business listings online. Not only will this help customers find you, but search engines such as Google will find your business more credible. </p>
<h2>Get Social </h2>
<p>Do you have a Facebook page, Twitter account, and Instagram account? If not, you’re missing out on free digital real estate. Be sure to <a href="/services/social-media-management/">set up a social media profile</a> for your business on the sites where it makes sense to do so. Not only do social media profiles show up in Google searches--increasing your visibility--but it also allows you to meet your customers where they’re at. </p>
<h2>Tell Google About Your Site</h2>
<p>If you don’t have a Google Search Console account set up, you need to do so right away. In your account, you can add a sitemap, which lists all of the pages on your site. This way, Google knows your site exists. After all, Google can’t list your site in the search results if it doesn’t know it’s there. Plus, Search Console will let you know if there are any issues with your site that can keep it from appearing on Google. </p>
<h2>Update Your Website</h2>
<p>You want to make sure that your website is up-to-date, for your customers' sake as much as search engines. Be sure your website has all of your current and correct information. It’s also a good idea to add new reviews or before and after images to your site for a personal touch. If your site has a blog, be sure to add new blog posts regularly. Nothing says a site is outdated like the most recent blog post being over a year old.</p>
