<h3>1. Small Businesses Just Starting Out</h3>
<p>Have you recently started your own small business, or have plans to? Congrats! One of the first things you should do for a new business is to have a professional website created. After all, your website is like your storefront on the internet, and first impressions matter. If you’re starting from scratch, there’s no better time to build a solid foundation for your online presence.</p>

Faster load times and an updated aesthetic aren’t just nice to have—they’re essential for staying competitive. Kite Media focuses on creating sites that look great, work on all devices (from computers to tablets to mobile phones), and are optimized for conversions. We’d be happy to revamp your existing design or create a modern design from scratch. </p>
<h3>4. Growing  or Rebranding Businesses</h3>
<p>As your business evolves, your website should too. Maybe you’ve added new services, moved locations, or undergone a complete rebrand. A new website ensures that your online presence reflects who you are today and helps customers understand everything you have to offer. If your website no longer reflects your current business, now is the time to change that! </p>
