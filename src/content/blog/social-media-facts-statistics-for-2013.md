<p>This <strong>social media facts &amp; statistics for 2013</strong> infographic was done by Digital Insights. It covers some of the most recent Social Media facts, figures, numbers and statistics of the past year. We have highlighted some of the most interesting stats below:</p>
<p>– <strong>74%</strong> of marketers believe <a href="http://www.facebook.com/KiteMedia" target="_blank" title="Like us on Facebook">Facebook</a> is important for their lead generation strategy<br/>
– <strong>28%</strong> of Retweets on <a href="http://twitter.com/Kite_Media" target="_blank" title="Follow Kite Media on Twitter">Twitter</a> are due to inclusion of “please RT!”<br/>
– <strong>40%</strong> of marketers use <a href="http://plus.google.com/110330985048826043123/" target="_blank" title="Kite Media is on Google+">Google+</a>, 70% desire to learn more and 67% plan to increase Google+ activities<br/>
– <strong>42%</strong> update their profile information regularly on <a href="http://www.linkedin.com/company/2217236" target="_blank" title="We're on LinkedIn">LinkedIn</a><br/>
– Every second <strong>8000</strong> users like a photo on <a href="http://instagram.com/kite_media" target="_blank" title="See us on Instagram">Instagram</a><br/>
– <strong>80%</strong> of total <a href="http://pinterest.com/thekitemedia/" target="_blank" title="Kite Media on Pinterest">Pinterest’s</a> pins are re-pins (good news for businesses using Pinterest – upload your images!)<br/>
– The growing power of mobile! <strong>4.2 billion</strong> people use mobile device to access social media networks<br/>
– Social media generates nearly <strong>double</strong> the marketing leads of trade shows, telemarketing or daily mail<br/>
– More than <strong>23%</strong> of marketers are investing in blogging and social media (seems pretty low still?)</p>
