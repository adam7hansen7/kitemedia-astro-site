<h2><b>SEO: The Never-Ending Battle</b></h2>
<p>Many small business owners consider their internet marketing budget to be a variable expense; one that, with time, might even be eliminated. As they start to gain a bigger web presence, they reconsider how much money they want to spend to advertise online, thinking they have reached a satisfactory level of exposure. What those owners don’t realize is that their growing web presence is a result of the continual efforts that are being focused on their online presence, day after day, month after month, which often comes in the form of paying an internet marketing company each month. After you stop performing SEO on a website, it will eventually start to fall in the rankings, as search engines no longer view it as a current business producing fresh content. It’s important to always be improving your site, and that may include paying a digital marketing company or in-house team to take care of your website for you.</p>
<p>That being said, of course, it should always be a priority to put your money where you can get the best results. If you are a brand new business, and your website hasn’t been around long, it may be worth it to dish out a little more money for highly targeted Pay-Per-Click advertising (Google Adwords) in combination with SEO to quickly and efficiently establish your new business' online presence. Likewise, if you find that you are doubling up on social media and posting nearly as much as your internet marketing company, it might be wise to rethink that expenditure. It’s up to you as the business owner, with the help of your internet marketing service provider, to make sure that the programs you are paying for are profitable. Never pull the plug on your SEO efforts because you think you are good enough as it is.</p>
