<p>Just like technology, digital marketing is changing every day. Consumers expect companies to reach them through their preferred channels. Good digital marketing requires a proactive and not reactive approach to changing trends in order to stay relevant and effective. Here are the top four digital marketing trends to expect in the new year. </p>
<h2>#1 ChatBots will take over customer service</h2>
<p>ChatBots are a part of the new movement towards artificial intelligence (AI). This software takes the place of immediate customer service and assists users in completing their goals. Typically chatbots interact with customers through chat windows and messaging but verbal interactions are possible as well. </p>
<p>The technology grows over time by internalizing more customer insights. This means AI is constantly evolving and becoming better at improving the customer experience. It’s clear the benefits chatbots provide:</p>
<p>✔ Instant response to customer questions</p>
<p>✔ 24/7 available service</p>
<p>✔ No need for breaks, vacation, or overtime pay</p>
<p>According to<a href="https://linchpinseo.com/chatbot-statistics-trends/#:~:text=Chatbot%20trends%20in%202021,-Experts%20believe%20that&amp;text=47%25%20of%20organizations%20are%20expected,services%2C%20and%20improve%20customer%20service."> Linchpin</a>, in 2021, “47% of organizations are expected to implement chatbots for customer support services, and 40% are expected to adopt virtual assistants.”</p>
<p>Chatbots are expected in today’s business world. According to <a href="http://www.oracle.com/us/technologies/mobile/chatbot-infographic-3672253.pdf">Oracle</a>, more than 50% of consumers anticipate that businesses are open 24/7. These AI messaging systems are popular among both baby boomers and millennials. If you do not have a chatbot for your website or Facebook page, it’s time to get one! Reach out to a <a href="/services/website-design/">website design</a> service to help implement a chatbot into your existing website. </p>

<h2>#2 Omnichannel marketing is the new normal</h2>
<p>Some of you may be wondering what “omnichannel” marketing even means. Omnichannel marketing is the practice of marketing across multiple platforms such as social media, email, apps, website blogs, and more.</p>
<p>This type of marketing will be critical moving forward into 2021. The COVID-19 pandemic shifted traditional marketing as most customers are now used to staying at home and spending more time online. For a company to reach its full audience, it must implement omnichannel marketing.</p>
<p>Although tactics may vary across channels, the messaging should be the same. Use a consistent voice and brand message across all channels. According to <a href="https://www.clickz.com/onmichannel-marketing-automation-statistics-for-2019/231381-2/231381/">ClickZ</a>, omnichannel marketing drives over three times as much engagement as a single-channel approach. Your investment in a <a href="/">digital marketing</a> service will be worth it.</p>
<p>The answer is simple, not only do customers expect omnichannel marketing but this type of marketing increases engagement, conversions, and sales.</p>

<h2>#4 Interactive content is expected</h2>
<p>Interactive content is just that, interactive. This means providing content that consumers can click on, swipe, or interact with online. Some examples of interactive content include:</p>
<p>✔ 360-degree videos</p>
<p>✔ Quizzes and polls</p>
<p>✔ Online product trials</p>
<p>✔ Content with share, like, or comment capabilities</p>
<p>✔ Contests</p>
<p>This type of content leverages advanced marketing technology to offer a more immersive experience. We live in an experience age, where customers no longer expect just great products or services but expect great experiences as well. Memorable and fun content helps customers to feel a part of your company’s brand.</p>

</blockquote>
