<p>AI website builders are everywhere right now. Type in a few prompts, pick a template, and in a few minutes you've got something that looks like a website. It's fast, it's cheap, and if you're a service business trying to keep costs down, it's tempting. After all, everyone knows a business needs a website. </p>
<p>If you're a carpet cleaner, a plumber, a landscaper, or another small business wondering whether you even need a web design agency anymore, here's what our <a href="/services/website-design/">web designers at Kite Media</a> have seen after years of building websites for service businesses like yours.</p>
<h2><b>1. AI Doesn't Know Your Business (or Your Customers)</b></h2>
<p>AI website builders are pattern-matchers. They're great at spitting out a generic layout that looks polished on the surface. What they're not good at is understanding the specific way people search for, evaluate, and choose a service business.</p>
<p>At <a href="/">Kite Media</a>, we've spent years working with hundreds of service businesses across the country (and Canada!). We know what information your customers actually want, how to get them to click on a contact form or tap to call you, and how to instill trust in your business. That's not something a prompt can replicate. It comes from doing this work, in this industry, over and over.</p>
<h2><b>2. A Website Should Generate Leads, Not Just Look Nice</b></h2>
<p>Plenty of AI-built sites look fine, great even. Looking fine isn't the goal, though. The goal is turning visitors into calls, form fills, and booked jobs.</p>
<p>That takes thinking through things an AI tool isn't built to think through:</p>
<ul>
<li>Where your calls-to-action actually belong on the page</li>
<li>What trust signals matter most to your specific audience</li>
<li>How your pages are structured so both people and search engines understand what you do and where you do it</li>
</ul>
<p>A website that isn't built with lead generation in mind is just a digital brochure, and brochures don't make the phone ring.</p>
<h2><b>3. You Need to Be Able to Update It </b></h2>
<p>A lot of AI-generated sites lock you into their platform, their editor, and their limitations. Want to add a new service page, swap out a photo, or update your hours? You're often stuck fighting the tool, or stuck paying someone else to do it. These sites often aren't built to be living, easy-to-update sites the way business websites need to be. </p>
<p>At Kite Media, we build on WordPress with an easy-to-use CMS, so you can go in and make changes or add new content whenever you need to, without worrying about breaking anything else on the site. And if you'd rather not touch it yourself, that's fine too. Our team can make updates for you, whether it's a quick text change or a whole new service page.</p>
<h2><b>4. We Handle Hosting, Domains, and the Technical Stuff</b></h2>
<p>Hosting, domains, security. None of it is glamorous, but all of it matters, and it's easy to get wrong if you're not paying attention or don't know what you're doing. We can take care of that side of things, so you're not left googling error messages because your site went down or your domain lapsed. </p>
<h2><b>5. AI Content Can Be Confidently Wrong</b></h2>
<p>It's not just the design that AI tools handle, it's often the words on the page, too, like the service descriptions, the location details, the claims about what you offer. And AI-generated content can be confidently wrong. It might get your business right. It might also invent a service you don't provide, misstate a detail about your industry, or phrase something in a way that raises red flags with customers or search engines.</p>
<p>When we write for your site, we make sure to collect all of the information from you, the business owner. If we don't know something, we’ll ask, not confidently make up inaccurate information about your business. Accuracy is a high priority for us, as we're sure it is for you. </p>
<h2><b>6. AI Sites Aren’t SEO and PPC-Ready</b></h2>
<p>A website is really just the foundation. Once it's live, the businesses that grow fastest are usually the ones that build on top of it. When you're ready, we also offer<a href="/services/search-engine-optimization/"> SEO</a> and <a href="/services/pay-per-click/">PPC</a> services, so your new site doesn't just sit there looking good. It shows up when your customers are searching, and it can start bringing in leads through paid campaigns, too.</p>
<p>Even if you don't take us up on our SEO services, each of our websites is built with the SEO basics–such as optimized content, image alt text, and meta descriptions–in mind, so your site hits the ground running. </p>
<h2><b>Your Website Should Work as Hard as You Do</b></h2>
<p>An AI website builder can get you a website fast and cheap. It can't get you years of experience with service businesses, a site actually designed to generate leads, the flexibility to grow your site over time, or a team handling the technical stuff behind the scenes.</p>
