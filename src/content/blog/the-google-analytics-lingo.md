<p>Go<img alt="The Google Analytics Glossary" height="160" loading="lazy" src="http://kitemediaworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/google-analytics-lingo.png" width="160"/>ogle Analytics is an online tool used to track the number of visits a website receives. A website may be linked to an analytics account that will track an extensive amount of information in regards to that site: Visits, % New Visits, Bounce Rate, Absolute Unique Visitors, location information of visitors, Avg. Time on Site, and many more variables.</p>
<p><strong>– Visits:</strong> The actual number of visits your site receives.<br/>
<strong>– Page Views:</strong> The total number of pages that the visitors to your site viewed.<br/>
<strong>– Pages/Visit:</strong> The average number of pages that each visitor to your site viewed.<br/>
<strong>– Bounce Rate:</strong> The percentage of single-page visits (i.e. visits in which the person left your site after viewing only the home page – they bounced). A bounce rate around 50% is normal.<br/>
<strong>– % New Visits:</strong> The percentage of your site’s visits that are new visitors, who have never visited your site. A high number of <strong>new</strong> visitors suggests that you are successful at driving traffic to your site while a high number of <strong>return</strong> visitors suggests that the site content is engaging enough for visitors to come back.<br/>
<strong>– Absolute Unique Visitors:</strong> This shows how many <strong>people</strong> came to your site. This report graphs <strong>people</strong> instead of visits.<br/>
<strong>– Traffic Sources:</strong> The different kinds of sources that send traffic to your site.</p>
<ul>
<li>– Direct Traffic: Visits from people who typed your site URL directly into their browser or clicked a bookmark.</li>
<li>– Referring Sites: Visits from people who clicked to your site from another website (i.e., Facebook, Twitter, Yelp, etc).</li>
<li>– Search Engines: Visits from people who clicked to your site from a search engine results page. Ideally this should be the highest source of visitors to your website. A high ratio of search engine visits denotes a well-optimized site that can easily be found online.</li>
</ul>
